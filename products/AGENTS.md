# PRODUCTS KNOWLEDGE BASE

## OVERVIEW

Sales, offer, and fulfillment materials for marketplace or client-facing work. Treat these as business artifacts.

## STRUCTURE

```
products/
├── coconala/     # Japanese marketplace listings and fulfillment
└── freelancer/   # English freelancer profile/materials
```

## WHERE TO LOOK

| Task | Location | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coconala offers | `coconala/listing-*.md` | Listing copy and package framing. |
| Fulfillment | `coconala/fulfillment.md` | Delivery procedure. |
| Freelancer profile | `freelancer/gig-profiles-en.md` | English positioning. |

## CONVENTIONS

- Separate draft copy from actual publication.
- Claims need evidence, examples, or clear assumptions.
- Keep offers aligned with what the OS can actually deliver automatically.

## ANTI-PATTERNS

- Do not publish, submit, bid, or message externally without approval.
- Do not promise results the current scripts cannot produce.
- Do not hide marketplace or advertising disclosure requirements.
