# DOCS KNOWLEDGE BASE

## OVERVIEW

Product, architecture, onboarding, and business-design documents. These docs define intent and constraints for implementation.

## WHERE TO LOOK

| Task | Location | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Architecture | `ARCHITECTURE.md` | 4 layers, least privilege, audit log. |
| Company strategy | `COMPANY_STRATEGY.md` | Business framing. |
| Onboarding/security | `ONBOARDING.md` | What can and cannot be shared. |
| Reception business | `RECEPTION_BUSINESS_DESIGN.md` | Domain design. |
| Reception lead generation | `RECEPTION_LEAD_GENERATION.md` | Lead workflow. |

## CONVENTIONS

- Docs are part of the source of truth for approval, spend, and security boundaries.
- Keep business language concise and evidence-based.
- Avoid copying credentials, personal IDs, bank login details, card numbers, or master passwords.

## ANTI-PATTERNS

- Do not turn draft strategy into executed external action.
- Do not change approval or money-handling policy as a wording cleanup.
- Do not add optimistic claims without measurements or clear assumptions.
